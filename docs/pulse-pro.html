AVGen-Bench: A Task-Driven Benchmark for Multi-Granular Evaluation of Text-to-Audio-Video Generation
Overview
Methodology
Results
Implications
AVGen-Bench introduces a task-driven benchmark for Text-to-Audio-Video generation, featuring high-quality prompts and a multi-granular evaluation framework.
⚡ Technical Methodology
AVGen-Bench employs a hybrid evaluation strategy that integrates lightweight specialist models with Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). This design leverages the complementary strengths of both paradigms: specialist models provide precise signal-level measurements, while MLLMs enable high-level semantic reasoning and holistic intent verification.
📊 Experimental Results
AVGen-Bench reveals a sharp gap between strong audio-visual aesthetics and weak fine-grained semantic control, highlighting critical challenges in text, speech, and physical reasoning. The evaluation framework identifies recurring failure modes, including speech content that is unintelligible or incorrect, environmental sounds that do not align with visual events, mismatched lip movements, incorrect musical notes, and violations of basic physical or causal logic.
🎯 Strategic Angle
This benchmark highlights a key gap for anyone building multimodal content tools. Fine-grained semantic alignment — not just visual quality — is the frontier. Products that solve the "semantic control" problem first will define the next generation of AI-generated media.
